این پرامپت رو به ChatGPT بده و بذار با استفاده از گفتگوهای قبلیت، فقط یک راز درباره خودت بهت بگه؛ چیزی که احتمالاً وقتی بشنویش، چند ثانیه با خودت می‌گی: «عه... راست میگه! من واقعاً این کارو می‌کنم!»
Find ONE hidden pattern about me that I may not have consciously noticed.
Use everything you genuinely remember from our previous conversations with me.
Do NOT give me a personality analysis.
Do NOT give me generic traits like "you're ambitious", "you're curious", "you overthink", or similar observations that could apply to almost anyone.
Instead, look for something subtle that appears repeatedly across my conversations, such as:
- a recurring choice I make
- a pattern in what I ask for
- something I consistently care about without explicitly saying it
- a contradiction between what I say and what I repeatedly do
- a preference that quietly appears in different contexts
- a recurring way I approach problems or make decisions
- something I repeatedly avoid, seek, refine, reject, or return to
- a connection between seemingly unrelated things I've talked about
The pattern must be supported by multiple pieces of evidence from our actual conversations.
Then tell me:
***🔎***
 THE SECRET
State the ONE hidden pattern in one clear sentence.
***🧩***
 HOW YOU NOTICED IT
Give 2–3 specific examples from our conversations that led you to this observation.
***💡***
 WHY I MAY HAVE MISSED IT
Explain briefly why this pattern could have been easy for me to overlook.
***🪞***
 WHAT IT MIGHT MEAN
Explain what this pattern could reveal about the way I think, choose, work, or respond to things.
IMPORTANT:
- Be specific.
- Be honest.
- Do not flatter me.
- Do not invent information.
- Do not make psychological or medical diagnoses.
- If you don't have enough evidence for a genuinely non-obvious pattern, say so instead of making one up.
- Choose only ONE observation.
- Make the observation feel like something I could realistically say: "Wait... I've actually been doing that."
