**OnionHop 3.7.1**
 (v3.7.1)
Adds a Copy button to saved bridges and SNI hosts, so you can rescan them without applying first.
### Added
- **Copy button in the saved library.** Each entry in Scanner > Saved now has a "Copy" button next to Apply and Delete. It copies just that bridge line or SNI host to the clipboard, so you can paste it into the bridge scanner or SNI scanner and rescan it directly. Previously you had to Apply an entry and copy it from another tab before you could rescan it.
